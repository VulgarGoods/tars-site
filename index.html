TARS Assistant

TARS Assistant is a small personal home-organizer built by and for one person, Michael. It runs on his own computer and helps him keep track of his family calendar, household bills, and email replies.

What it does with Google data

  • Google Calendar (read-only): reads his own calendars to prepare a short daily agenda.
  • Gmail (read, and create drafts): reads his own messages so he can see what needs a reply, and can save a reply draft in his own Drafts folder. It never sends email.

TARS Assistant is not a public service. It has one user, its owner, and is not offered to anyone else.
